Questions people ask first

What does HR360 TechX do?

HR360 TechX keeps employee records, attendance, leave requests, and payroll runs in one workspace. Employees open their own payslips. Managers approve time and leave.

Does HR360 TechX file payroll taxes?

No. HR360 TechX records payroll runs, payslips, time, and leave. It does not file federal or state payroll taxes and it does not pay employees for you.

How much does HR360 TechX cost?

Pricing is quoted for your team. See what the quote covers, then book a demo and the 360techx team replies with a price for the workspace you need.

Where do employees sign in?

Employees sign in at their company address, for example yourcompany.hr360techx.com. The public sign-in page is hr360techx.com/login.
