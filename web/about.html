About

HR360 TechX is the product. 360techx is the company.

The app, the sign-in page, and this website use the product name HR360 TechX. Email, support, and the company behind the product use 360techx. Both names refer to the same workforce workspace.

What the company ships

360techx builds HR360 TechX for teams that want employee records, attendance, leave, and payroll runs in one workspace. Each customer company has its own sign-in address on hr360techx.com.

The product records the work. It does not file federal or state payroll taxes, and it does not pay employees for you. That limit is stated on the product pages so a buyer does not mistake it for a payroll bureau.

Guides for US employers live under the US payroll hub. They explain forms and deadlines in plain language. They are general information, not tax or legal advice.
