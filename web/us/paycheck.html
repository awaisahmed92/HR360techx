This estimate follows Worksheet 1A and the 2026 Percentage Method tables in IRS Publication 15-T for a 2020 or later Form W-4. It assumes no extra withholding, no Step 3 credits, and no other income on Step 4. It is not a filing.

Federal method: Publication 15-T (2026). Social Security is 6.2% of wages until the $184,500 wage base. Medicare is 1.45%, plus 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax on wages over $200,000 in the year. Source: Publication 15 (2026).

Texas and Florida have no personal income-tax withholding. For every other state in the list, use the withholding pages linked from that state guide. This tool does not apply state brackets.
