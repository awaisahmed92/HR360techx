Time and attendance for small business payroll

Time and attendance in HR360 TechX covers attendance, shifts, timesheets, and manager approvals. Those hours can sit next to the payroll run. They are not a certified time clock for every state overtime rule.

Why hours matter

Federal overtime is generally time and a half after 40 hours in a workweek. California also uses daily overtime. The overtime calculator estimates those two cases and links the state pages for local rules.

Restaurants, construction crews, and home-care shifts are the cases where a missed punch changes the paycheck. Industry notes are on the restaurant, construction, and home care pages.

This is general information for employers, not tax, legal, or payroll-filing advice. Rates and deadlines change. Confirm them with the cited agency before you pay people or file a return. HR360 TechX records payroll runs, payslips, time, and leave. It does not file Form 941, Form 940, W-2s, or 1099s.
