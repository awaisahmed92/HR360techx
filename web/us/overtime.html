Overtime calculator

The federal estimate pays straight time for the first 40 hours in the workweek and time and a half after that. The California option is a one-day estimate only.

Federal source: DOL overtime. California daily overtime source: DIR overtime FAQ. This calculator does not combine a long day with a long week, and it ignores seventh-day rules.
