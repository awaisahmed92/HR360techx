Payroll data, tax filing, and support

HR360 TechX keeps payroll runs, payslips, time, and leave in the employer’s workspace. The employer, not HR360 TechX, files federal and state payroll taxes and is responsible for the accuracy of those returns.

Who files

IRS Publication 15 tells employers they remain responsible for withholding, depositing, and filing even when a third party helps. HR360 TechX is not that filing agent. It does not deposit taxes, file Form 941 or Form 940, or issue W-2s and 1099s. Source: Publication 15 (2026), outsourcing discussion.

What this page does not claim

  • No SOC 2 report is stated here, and none is on file in this product’s public pages.
  • No accuracy guarantee is offered for tax calculations. The calculators on this site are estimates.
  • No street address or US phone number is published here, because those details are not in the product pages this site is allowed to quote.

The HR360 TechX marketing page includes a support chat. Hours are whatever that chat shows. They are not restated here.
