Payroll for restaurants

Restaurants pay people across split shifts, tips, and short weeks. Federal law still uses a 40-hour workweek for overtime, and several states limit the tip credit. HR360 TechX can store shifts, timesheets, leave, and a payroll run. It does not calculate a tip credit or file restaurant withholding.

Related tools: overtime calculator and employer cost calculator. State registration steps start at the state pages.

This is general information for employers, not tax, legal, or payroll-filing advice. Rates and deadlines change. Confirm them with the cited agency before you pay people or file a return. HR360 TechX records payroll runs, payslips, time, and leave. It does not file Form 941, Form 940, W-2s, or 1099s.
