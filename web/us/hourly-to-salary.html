Hourly to salary converter

Annual pay here is the hourly rate times hours per week times 52. It does not withhold taxes.

A person paid for fewer than 52 weeks, or paid overtime, will not match this figure. Use the overtime calculator when hours pass 40 in a workweek.

This is general information for employers, not tax, legal, or payroll-filing advice. Rates and deadlines change. Confirm them with the cited agency before you pay people or file a return.
