HR360 TechX vs ADP

ADP is a full-service payroll and HR vendor that can calculate pay, deposit taxes, and file employment returns. HR360 TechX is online payroll software from 360techx for people, time, leave, and payroll runs — it does not file Form 941 or deposit payroll taxes.

Use this page to decide whether you need a bureau that files for you, or a workforce workspace that keeps the run next to attendance and leave.

What is different

  • ADP’s payroll products are built around paying employees and filing payroll taxes. HR360 TechX does not file Form 941, Form 940, W-2s, or 1099s.
  • HR360 TechX payroll setup includes a USA payroll type and weekly, biweekly, and monthly schedules. The employer still registers with the IRS and the state.
  • Time, attendance, and leave live in the same workspace as the payroll run. That does not replace ADP’s tax filing or direct deposit service.

When to choose which

Choose ADP (or another bureau) if you want a vendor to file and deposit payroll taxes. Use HR360 TechX if you want the people, time, leave, and payroll-run record in one place and you will file the returns yourself or through a separate filer.
