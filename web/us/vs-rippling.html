HR360 TechX vs Rippling

Rippling is a broader HR and IT platform that can include payroll with tax filing. HR360 TechX is online payroll software from 360techx focused on employee records, attendance, leave, and payroll runs — without filing US payroll taxes.

The products overlap on people and pay, but they solve different filing jobs.

What is different

  • Rippling’s payroll path is built to calculate, deposit, and file where you subscribe to that service. HR360 TechX does not file Form 941, Form 940, W-2s, or 1099s.
  • HR360 TechX keeps attendance and leave on the same employee as the payroll run, with weekly, biweekly, and monthly schedules. The employer still registers with the IRS and the state.
  • Rippling also sells device and app management. HR360 TechX does not replace an IT asset platform.

When to choose which

Choose Rippling if you want a single vendor for HR, IT, and tax-filing payroll. Use HR360 TechX if you want a focused workforce workspace for people, time, leave, and payroll runs, and you will file returns yourself or through a separate filer.
