Pricing

A quote for the workspace, not a public price grid

HR360 TechX does not list a dollar price on this site. The 360techx team quotes from the size of the team and the parts of the workspace you will use. Book a demo and the reply includes the price.

Book a demo info@360techx.com

What the quote covers

  • Employee records, roles, and the organization chart.
  • Attendance, shifts, timesheets, and approvals.
  • Leave requests and manager approvals.
  • Payroll runs and payslips for weekly, biweekly, or monthly schedules, plus off-cycle runs.
  • Employee self-service for a person's own requests and payslips.

Tell us the headcount and which of those you need. If you only want records and leave, say that. The quote should match the workspace, not a bundle you will not open.

What a demo is for

A demo is a look at payroll, attendance, and leave with your process, plus a written price afterward. It is not a free tax-filing service and it is not a commitment to buy on the call.

Send the request from Book a demo. It is saved with the other HR360 messages so the team can reply to the email you give.
